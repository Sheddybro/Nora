# NorrisBeauty premium design system v0.1

## Brand palette
Ivory #FAF7F2, Champagne #E8D8BE, Rose Gold #B76E79, Charcoal #252329, White #FFFFFF.
Use rose gold for accents, not all body text. Maintain WCAG AA contrast; adjust accessible text tones where required.

## Visual principles
Premium but restrained, spacious layouts, consistent 8px spacing rhythm, subtle elevation, 12–20px corner radii, clear information hierarchy, excellent data-table density and visible interaction feedback.

## Components
App shell, collapsible sidebar, topbar, role dashboard cards, metric trends, tables, filters, product tiles, product gallery, POS cart, barcode input, form fields, dialogs, toasts, skeletons, empty states, error states, notifications, breadcrumbs, pagination, dark-mode switch.

## Responsive design
390px phone, 768px tablet, 1440px desktop screenshot baselines. No horizontal overflow except intentionally scrollable tables. Touch targets at least 44px where feasible.

## Dark mode
Use semantic tokens rather than reversing colors; preserve contrast and chart readability.

## Design assets to create later
Original branded mockups for owner dashboard, staff dashboard, products, category browser, POS, sales history, purchase orders, inventory transfers, reports, user management, login, and mobile equivalents. Use licensed or generated product photography with descriptive filenames and asset manifest. Inspiration images are not bundled as owned assets.
