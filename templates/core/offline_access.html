Offline catalog and stock

Save a small, encrypted, read-only snapshot while online. POS, stock changes, and all other business actions still need a live connection.

What is saved

Active product names, categories, SKUs and barcodes, plus stock quantities by location and reorder points. Prices, costs, customers, sales, reports, staff, suppliers, images, passwords, and API responses are not saved.

The snapshot expires after 24 hours, is locked after 5 minutes idle, and remains readable offline until it expires even if access is revoked while the device is disconnected.

At least 12 characters. Do not reuse your website password. The passphrase and encryption key are not stored.
